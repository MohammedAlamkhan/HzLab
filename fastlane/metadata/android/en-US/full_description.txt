HzLab is a modern chromatic tuner and pitch visualiser for Android, built for current 64-bit phones and tablets.

Its modern dark interface has a compact app header, labelled bottom navigation and card-based settings, while keeping the tuning workflow fast and uncluttered.

It brings five complementary tuning displays into one offline app:

• Instrument — a radial strobe with string indicators for the selected tuning. Upper harmonics are folded back to the intended string, so a bright harmonic does not appear as an unrelated high note.
• Dial & Strobe — a cent scale with a moving needle and a linear strobe. Flat moves left; sharp moves right.
• Spectrogram — a scrolling 240-bin pitch display with 5-cent resolution, a live cents readout, and enough detail to see vibrato and microtonal drift.
• Chords — a 12 by 6 grid for major, major 7th, dominant 7th, minor, minor 7th and diminished chords, with temporal smoothing and root/bass weighting.
• Big Strobe — a large single-strobe display for small screens and hands-free tuning.

Advanced DSP controls let you adapt the tuner to your instrument and room: noise gate, clarity threshold, harmonic search depth, pitch smoothing, spectrogram contrast and speed, chord gate and chord smoothing. Defaults preserve the original behaviour.

Tunings and temperaments:

• 52 built-in instrument tunings, including guitar, bass, violin, fiddle, mandolin, ukulele, banjo, bouzouki and pedal steel.
• 17 temperaments, including equal temperament and historical temperaments such as Kirnberger, Meantone, Pythagorean, Valotti and Young.
• Custom instruments with up to 12 strings or notes.
• Custom 12-note temperaments with editable cent offsets.
• A4 reference from 220 to 880 Hz in 0.1 Hz steps, sharp/flat note names and selectable in-tune sensitivity.

Privacy and design:

• Audio is processed entirely on the device. HzLab does not record, save, upload or share microphone audio.
• No ads, analytics, trackers, accounts or network permission.
• Pure Java, with no native libraries or ABI-specific binaries.
• Supports Android 6.0 and newer, including 64-bit-only devices.
